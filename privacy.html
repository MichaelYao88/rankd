Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Rankd at ranki.gg (the "site"). Rankd is a free, ad-supported daily puzzle game made by Michael Yao ("we", "us"). The short version: you can play without an account, and your streak and progress stay in your own browser. Signing in is optional. A finished Daily or Archive puzzle also sends an anonymous score summary so community percentages can be counted. That summary can include a coarse country (for example Taiwan or the United States) worked out from the connection. We do not store your IP address. The ads on the site are provided by Google AdSense, and anonymous product analytics by PostHog, both described below.

Accounts (optional)

You don't need an account to play. If you want one, the sign-in screen offers Continue with Google and Continue with email. Email sign-in sends a magic link from Supabase's built-in email. We store the account with Supabase: your email address, and, when you use Google, the name and profile picture Google sends. A challenge link uses that signed-in display name. If there is no display name, the link says "Your friend". Your email address is never used as the challenge name. A link that already contains a name still shows that name when someone opens it. The name lives only in that link. It is not sent to our database or to the ads and analytics vendors.

The sign-in session is saved in this browser under rankd.supabase.auth. That key is only for Ranki, so it does not replace a session from another game on the same host. Sign out removes it. Clear all data removes it too, and signs you out. Clearing data does not wipe the whole browser store, so other projects on this host keep their own data.

Premium is one monthly subscription for Ranki and WhereIsShe. Checkout is hosted by Lemon Squeezy. We do not receive your card number. We store whether Premium is on, until when, and whether it came from Ranki or WhereIsShe. That row is readable only by the signed-in account it belongs to. A subscription bought on WhereIsShe is applied here when you sign in with the same confirmed email.

The optional "Submit your own puzzle" form on the home page asks for a question, and you can add an optional name or handle. If you send a suggestion, we store the question or stat, any countries you list, an optional source link, and that optional name. We also store a short browser label and, when product analytics is on, the same random analytics id already used for PostHog, so repeated junk is easier to spot. That id is not an account. Suggestions are insert-only from the site: the page cannot read them back.

When you finish a Daily or Archive puzzle, the site sends one anonymous row to our database (hosted by Supabase): the puzzle id, the puzzle's date, whether it was Daily or Archive, the puzzle type, whether you solved it, whether that clear was perfect, and a short summary of your marks or guesses, including what you submitted on the first try and, if you tried again, the second try. The row has no name, email or account id. Practice, Studio and Loading Zone preview (?preview=) plays are not sent. Your streak, settings and unfinished games stay in this browser and are not part of that row.

To group those anonymous rows ("what players in your country guessed first"), our server reads the country of the connection. If the host already provides a country code, that is used. Otherwise the server asks ipwho.is for the country code only and then discards the IP. The database stores an ISO country code such as TW or US, not the IP address, city, or precise location. If the country can't be determined, the row is stored without one. The same lookup may run when the results panel loads, and the code is cached in this browser for a few hours (rankd.country.v1, in session and local storage) so the panel can pick the matching group. That cache is removed when you clear Rankd data. It is not an account, and it is not sent to the ads or product-analytics vendors as part of this feature.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to run the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Product analytics (PostHog)

Rankd uses PostHog for anonymous product analytics. We record that a round started, that a round finished (whether you solved it, and how many guesses it took), that you opened the comparison with other players, that you clicked a share button, that you created or opened a challenge link, or that a puzzle suggestion was sent (puzzle_submitted). An event can include the mode (daily, archive or practice) and the puzzle type. It does not include your name, email, or anything you type, including your guesses and puzzle suggestions. PostHog may store a random identifier in your browser so those events can be counted; that identifier is not an account. Studio and Loading Zone preview (?preview=) are not tracked.

What is stored in your browser (localStorage)

To remember your progress, Rankd saves a few items in your browser's storage on your device. Puzzle progress, stats and settings stay on the device. The sign-in session is sent only to Ranki's Supabase project so the account stays signed in:

  • rankd.results.v2: your progress and results for daily, archive and practice puzzles
  • rankd.stats.v2: games played, solved, current and best streak, and your score distribution
  • rankd.crosspromo.v1: whether you dismissed the WhereIsShe suggestion, and when it was last shown
  • rankd.settings.v1: your settings (light or dark theme, streamer mode)
  • rankd.country.v1: the coarse country code used to show community results for your country (no IP address)
  • rankd.custom.v2: only if you load your own puzzle spreadsheet
  • rankd.supabase.auth: your Ranki sign-in session, if you created an account
  • rankd.premium.intent: a short-lived flag (session storage) so checkout can reopen after you return from sign-in. It is not Premium itself

An old key, rankd.premium.v1, is ignored. Putting a value there does not turn Premium on.

You can delete all of these at any time: open the menu (⚙️), go to Settings → Your data → Clear all data, and confirm. This removes every Rankd item (anything beginning with rankd.) from your browser and resets the game. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, see How Google uses data from sites that use our services.

If you choose not to receive personalised ads, you may still see ads, but they will be based on general factors such as the page you're on or your approximate location rather than your interests. Ad blockers prevent ads from loading, and the game works the same without them. Streamer mode (Settings) hides all ad space and stops the ad script from loading at all.

Third-party vendors

Google AdSense may work with other certified ad vendors and ad networks to fill ad space. These third parties may also use cookies, as described in Google's policies. We don't control these cookies and don't receive the data they collect. A list of the ad technology providers Google works with is available through Google's ad technology providers page.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ads are shown only in line with your choices: you are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before cookies are used for personalised advertising. You can refuse, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy and cookie settings link offered by the consent message. Where you don't consent, only non-personalised (or limited) ads are shown. Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to the use of your personal data. Requests about advertising data are best directed to Google via the links above. For the anonymous play row or the product-analytics events described above, or anything else, you can contact us.

Children

Rankd is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Don't create an account for a child. Some puzzles cover adult subjects such as crime and health. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site, contact us and we will help remove it.

External links

Puzzles link to the original data sources (for example the WHO, the World Bank or national statistics offices). Those sites have their own privacy policies.
