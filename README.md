# Rankd: daily stereotype-busting stats game (prototype)

There are two puzzle types, mixed into one daily rotation:

* **Rank it:** drag 3–5 countries or groups into order for one stat, then lock it in. Cards in the right spot turn
  🟩 green and lock in place; wrong ones turn 🟥 red and shake. Rearrange the rest and lock in again, up to
  `CONFIG.rankMaxTries` (5) tries, after which the answer is revealed. Re-locking an unchanged order is refused.
  Share text: `Rankd #015 🔪 solved in 3 tries` plus one 🟩/🟥 row per try (or `X/5`).
  *Values-visible* variant: all the real values are listed in ONE separate column box beside the cards
  (sorted, one value per row, aligned with the card rows), never on the draggable cards; after the reveal each card shows its own value.
* **🖐️ Guess the top 5:** name the countries in a stat's top 5. Type a country, pick it from the suggestions (this only
  fills the box), then press **Guess** or Enter. Hits are free; you can miss 10 times (`max_misses`). Each guess
  shows ✅ hit (its rank 1–5) or ⬜ miss (its real world rank of N and value). The game ends when all 5 are found or
  the misses run out.
  * Score = found (X/5), plus a secondary *closeness* stat: the **average world rank of all your guesses** (lower = better).
  * Share: `Rankd #016 🖐️ Guess the top 5: Found 3/5 · avg rank #9` (or `Found 5/5 with 3 misses · avg rank #4`)
    plus the 🟩/⬜ guess sequence (10 per row).
  * The blue card shows the #1–#5 slots (found countries fill their slot) and a **Last miss** line (e.g. `Poland · #6 of 187`)
    that is replaced by each new miss. The full guess list is below the card.
  * **Hints** unlock after misses **2, 4, 6, 8 and 9**. Each hint is ONE trait about ONE top-5 country you haven't found:
    the highest-ranked unfound country gets its next unused hint (`Hint for #3: It's in Europe.`). Found it? Later hints
    move to the next unfound country. Each country has a bank of 5 hints, vague to specific: continent → population band →
    GDP per person band → capital's first letter (or coastline/landlocked) → first letter of its name. Banks live in the
    `hint_bank` sheet (auto_hint generated by build.py from `country_info`; type into `override_hint` to change one).
  * Search suggestions never follow the data order: an empty box shows a shuffled sample (seeded per session), and typed
    matches are sorted by match quality, then alphabetically.

## Run it
* **Easiest:** double-click `index.html`. It works from `file://` with no server and no internet,
  because SortableJS and SheetJS are bundled in `vendor/`.
* Or serve it: `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000

## Features
* **Today / Archive / Practice** tabs.
  * The daily puzzle number counts the days since `CONFIG.dailyEpoch` in `app.js`. It is currently `2026-10-06`,
    so the prototype's Archive has two weeks of puzzles to show. **Set it to your real launch date.**
  * The Archive lists today and every past daily with your result. Future dailies are never listed, and a future
    `#d=` link is refused. Archive plays don't count toward your streak or stats.
* **Interstitial ad:** this only appears on Archive and Practice puzzles. It shows between submitting and the reveal,
  with a 5-second skip countdown. Today's daily never gets one (`CONFIG.interstitialOnDaily`).
* **Single top banner ad.** It sits under the header, where fermi.gg puts its leaderboard ad. It's a labeled
  placeholder, and comments in `index.html` show where the AdSense code goes.
* **Sharing:** there are two buttons.
  * *Share today's puzzle* is spoiler-free: the question plus a deep link.
  * *Share my result* includes the score, or the number of tries, plus the emoji grid. For example:
    `Rankd #016 🖐️ Guess the top 5: Found 3/5 · avg rank #9`.
  * Links deep-link to the puzzle: `#d=YYYY-MM-DD` for a daily or archive puzzle, `#p=<puzzle_id>` for practice,
    and `#archive` for the Archive.
  * Set `CONFIG.siteUrl` once the site has a domain. From `file://` a placeholder URL is used.
* **Settings** (☰ → Settings):
  * Theme: System (the default) / Light / Dark. It's saved in the browser, and the 🌙/☀️ button in the header toggles it too.
  * Show values while guessing: Puzzle default / Always / Never. The 👁 chip on each Rank-it puzzle toggles it as well.
  * Load spreadsheet, and reset to the built-in puzzles.
* **"What % of players got it right":** this is a placeholder only, and no player data exists yet. The backend plugs in
  at `CommunityStats.submit()` / `CommunityStats.fetch()` near the top of `app.js` (Supabase or a Cloudflare Worker + D1).
* Your results, streak and stats are kept in `localStorage`.

## Editing puzzles (`puzzles.xlsx`)
| sheet | one row per | columns |
|---|---|---|
| `puzzles` (Rank it) | item | puzzle_id, daily_order, puzzle_emoji, question, stat_label, unit, higher_is (`top`/`bottom`), values_visible (`yes`/`no`), item_name, item_emoji, value, data_year, source_name, source_url, fun_fact, global_rank, global_total, global_scope, art, support_url |
| `top5_puzzles` (Guess the top 5) | puzzle | puzzle_id, daily_order, puzzle_emoji, question, stat_label, unit, data_from (a `country_lists` list id; blank = own id), max_misses, data_year, source_name, source_url, fun_fact, art, support_url |
| `country_lists` | country, per list | puzzle_id, country, iso3, emoji, value, year |
| `country_info` | country | iso3, country, region, continent, capital, landlocked, part_of, population, population_year, gdp_per_capita_usd, gdp_year, source (feeds the hint bank) |
| `hint_bank` | hint | puzzle_id, rank, country, iso3, hint_no (1–5), auto_hint, override_hint (wins if filled), source |

* In `puzzles`, the puzzle-level columns are read from the **first row** of each puzzle.
* For Top 5 puzzles the top 5 = the 5 highest values in the list; ranks are computed automatically (ties share a rank).
* `daily_order` sets the daily rotation across both types, lowest first. The rotation repeats when it runs out.
* The **HOW TO EDIT** tab in the workbook explains every column.

### Using an edited sheet
1. **Play it right away:** ☰ → Settings → **Load spreadsheet** and pick the `.xlsx`. You can also select one or more of
   the CSV copies together (`puzzles.csv`, `top5_puzzles.csv`, `country_lists.csv`, `hint_bank.csv`); the sheet type is
   detected from the headers. (An old `guess_data` list is still accepted as `country_lists`; old `guess_puzzles`/`hints` sheets are ignored.)
2. **Make it the built-in default:**
   ```
   pip install openpyxl      # once
   python3 build.py          # puzzles.xlsx -> puzzles-data.js + the CSV copies (fills in missing hint_bank rows)
   ```
   `python3 build.py --reset` rebuilds the workbook from the defaults in `build.py` plus `seed/*.csv`
   (`v6_puzzles`, `v7_puzzles`, `v8_puzzles`, `v9_puzzles`, `v7_top5_puzzles`, `country_lists`, `country_info`) and applies `SCHEDULE`. It **overwrites your edits**.

  grapher CSVs and the Wikipedia UNODC rape table). Each value has its year and real world rank #N of M. Scheduled
  Dec 2 2026 (#058) to Jan 1 2027 (#088). Earlier dates are unchanged.
* **6-item Rank it:** a puzzle can have 6 rows. Rows get more compact (`.board.n6`), so all 6 cards and the values column
  fit on a 390px phone. There are 5 six-item puzzles: homicide-c, fertility-c, prison-b, life-gap, military-b.
* **Top 5 ties:** the board always has exactly 5 slots. A tie at #5 uses the optional `tiebreak` column (unrounded
  value) in country_lists. With no tiebreak it becomes one shared #5 slot ("South Korea = Indonesia" for K-pop):
  either country counts, and the reveal notes the tie.

## Data rules
Every number and every hint must come from a real source with its URL in the sheet. Don't invent or estimate values.
The food country lists are FAO Food Balance "food available for consumption" figures (kg per person per year,
2023, or 2022 where 2023 is missing), downloaded from Our World in Data's Global Food explorer. They measure supply,
not what people actually eat, and don't account for household waste.
