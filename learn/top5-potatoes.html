Potato-eaters

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who eats the most potatoes per person?

This ranki.gg Rank it uses one measure: Potatoes available for consumption per person, FAO food balance, in kg/yr per capita. The countries on the card are Belarus, Ukraine, Poland, Ireland, United States, India. You do not have to recall the numbers from memory. They sit in a column. The job is to match each country to the value that belongs to it, then lock the order in. Five tries. A card in the right spot locks. A card in the wrong spot turns red so you can move it.

Belarus eats 166.9 kg of potatoes per person a year, ahead of Ukraine (147.0 kg). Ireland, the country people name first, is only #10 at 85.3 kg, and India is #90. Potatoes are a staple crop and a staple food in Belarus. The figure is FAO food available for consumption, not what is eaten at the table.
