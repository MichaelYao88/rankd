Cocoa consumption

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Kilograms per person of cocoa beans and products available for food in 2023. FAOSTAT calls this food supply; the game uses it as a proxy for consumption.

The measure is Cocoa supply, in kg/yr per person, year 2023. The source note stored with the series says: FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets, 2023: Cocoa Beans and products, food supply quantity (kg/capita/yr). FAO flag E (estimated). Countries that grind cocoa and export the chocolate can show a low figure, because the cocoa does not stay for residents.
