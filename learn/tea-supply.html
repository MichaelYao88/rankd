Tea and mate consumption

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Kilograms per person of tea, including yerba mate, available for food in 2023. FAOSTAT calls this food supply; the game uses it as a proxy for consumption.

The measure is Tea and mate supply, in kg/yr per person, year 2023. The source note stored with the series says: FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets, 2023: Tea (including mate), food supply quantity (kg/capita/yr). FAO flag E (estimated). The item includes yerba mate. A grower's high number is supply available for food, not a count of cups.

Contrasts already written for Guess the number

Guess the number shows one country's real value and asks you to type another. These fact lines are the ones stored with the pairs. They are not a new claim.

Turkey is 19.98 and United Kingdom is 1.23. Britain is the tea country in the stereotype. Turkey's figure is the leaf that stays for people to drink, and tea is an all-day drink there. Morocco is 1.62 and France is 0.21. France barely keeps any tea. In Morocco, mint tea is the default drink, so the supply is several times higher. Ireland is 1.46 and United Kingdom is 1.23. Ireland's supply is higher than Britain's. The British cup is the famous one, not the larger account. United Kingdom is 1.23 and United States is 0.5. The UK is ahead of the US, and the gap is real, but it is not a different order of magnitude.
