How many time zones a country has

This is a Tier it, not a rank from highest to lowest. Several countries can share a tier. The measure is Time zones, including dependent territories. The tiers on this card are 10 or more, 5 to 9, 2 to 4, 1. You drop each country into a tier and lock it in. A right tier locks. A wrong one turns red. You get five tries.

France has 12 time zones because overseas territories count, and would have 13 only if its Antarctic claim were included. The United States has 11, the same as Russia, once Baker Island and Wake Island are counted, not the four zones of the mainland. The United Kingdom has 9, the same as Australia, almost all of them outside Great Britain. Kiribati has 3, including UTC+14, and South Africa has 2 only because of the Prince Edward Islands. China has 1. No country on the list has 6, 7, or 8, and the only 10 is Antarctica, which is not a country.
