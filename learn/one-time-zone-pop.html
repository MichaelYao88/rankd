The fact says India is ahead of China, and that both keep a single legal time. China's row is UTC+8 because unofficial Xinjiang Time is not counted. Pakistan is third. The United States is off this list because the same Wikipedia table gives it 11 zones once Baker Island and Wake Island are included. Ethiopia is described as larger than Japan and just missing this six. Nigeria and Bangladesh are the fourth and fifth names on the card. Population is the World Bank total for 2024. A country is included only when the Wikipedia time-zone table says 1.

The source line on the puzzle is: World Bank, Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL), 2024, via country_attributes.csv. Wikipedia, List of time zones by country. The number is the table's own count of standard UTC offsets. Dependent territories are included, Antarctic claims are not, and daylight saving is not an extra zone. Unofficial offsets count only when the table lists them, so Australia includes UTC+08:45 and China does not include Xinjiang Time. France is 12, not 13. A country is on this list only when that table says 1. The puzzle does not add a separate caveat beyond that source line and the fact above. ranki.gg does not invent a value that is missing from that source. If two countries were so close that the order would depend on rounding, they would not be the pair the card asks you to separate.
