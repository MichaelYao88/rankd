How many do they count to with one hand?

This is a Tier it, not a rank from highest to lowest. Several countries can share a tier. The measure is Numbers one hand can show. The tiers on this card are 12+, Counts to 10, Counts to 5. You drop each country into a tier and lock it in. A right tier locks. A wrong one turns red. You get five tries.

India counts the lines between finger segments, four to a finger, so one hand reaches 20. China counts to five like the United States, then shows six through ten as gestures on that same hand: six is the thumb and the pinky. The United Kingdom runs from thumb to pinky, the United States from index finger to thumb, and Iran starts at the pinky, so each of those stops at five. The article does not name Pakistan, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Turkey, or Egypt.
