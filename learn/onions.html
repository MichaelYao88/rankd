Who eats the most onions per person?

This ranki.gg Rank it uses one measure: Onions available for consumption per person, FAO food balance, in kg/yr per capita. The countries on the card are Tajikistan, Senegal, India, United States, Philippines. You do not have to recall the numbers from memory. They sit in a column. The job is to match each country to the value that belongs to it, then lock the order in. Five tries. A card in the right spot locks. A card in the wrong spot turns red so you can move it.
