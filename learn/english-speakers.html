Not Just the UK

English speakers, leaving out the United States

This ranki.gg Rank it uses one measure: People who speak English, in English speakers, mixed. The countries on the card are India, Nigeria, Pakistan, Indonesia, Philippines. You do not have to recall the numbers from memory. They sit in a column. The job is to match each country to the value that belongs to it, then lock the order in. Five tries. A card in the right spot locks. A card in the wrong spot turns red so you can move it.

India has 269.8 million English speakers on this list, just behind the United States at 299.3 million, which is left off. Nigeria (125.0 million) has more than Pakistan (108.0 million). Indonesia (86.0 million) has more than the Philippines (70.1 million) and the United Kingdom (63.7 million), so the UK does not make this five. The counts are people who say they speak English, and the census years differ.
