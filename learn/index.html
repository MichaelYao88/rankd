Learn the numbers

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Short explainers for ranki.gg puzzles that stay on the safe side of the schedule. Every figure is copied from the puzzle or the series already in the game.

These pages do not add a dataset, a country, or a cause that the game's own fact and source line do not already state. Sensitive topics are not written up here. They stay in the daily game only after the AdSense-safe date set in the app, and they are not linked from this index.

Each explainer has the countries on that card or the top of that series, the unit, the source, and a link that opens the puzzle. Rank it pages walk the order from high to low. Tier it pages name who shares a tier. The quiz page repeats each card's fact. Guess the number pages use the FAO and World Bank series stored for tea, coffee, and cocoa.
